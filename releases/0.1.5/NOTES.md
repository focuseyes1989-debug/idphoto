ID Photo Studio 0.1.5

- Add Adjustments > Auto Retouch with strength, Before/After, Apply, Cancel and Undo. Conservative skin-color smoothing preserves facial geometry.
- Add Blur, Sharpen and Smudge soft brush tools in the left toolbar with size (1-2000 canvas pixels), strength and brush cursor.
- Cache brush results and process new stroke segments incrementally for faster painting.
- Add Photo Zoom: click/Alt-click, Ctrl+wheel, drag to pan and Fit to canvas. Viewing zoom does not change crop or export dimensions.
- Preserve per-photo retouch and brush edits in projects, Undo/Redo, full-resolution export and printing.
- Align brush tools in the toolbar and add color icons in the Tools menu.

Retouch is skin-color based and can affect similarly colored clothing. Brush and retouch preview results may differ slightly from full-resolution rendering.
